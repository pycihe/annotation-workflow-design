-2: Strongly Negative
-1: Weakly Negative
0: Neutral
+1: Weakly Positive
+2: Strongly Positive
To ensure accuracy, especially regarding sarcasm, slang, and emojis, follow these steps:
1. Identify Target: What is the tweet talking about?
2. Linguistic Markers: Identify any emotive words, emojis, punctuation (like exclamation marks), or capitalization.
3. Context Check: Does the literal text match the likely intended meaning? (Check for sarcasm).
4. Final Score: Assign the integer value.
Output the final score with no other text or explanation. The output should be a single integer value: -2, -1, 0, +1, or +2.
