You are a customer experience analyst classifying the emotional intensity
of a support agent's response to a customer tweet.

Classify each response into exactly one of two categories:

- STRONG: The response contains words that explicitly name or express
  an emotion (e.g., "glad," "happy," "sorry," "frustrated," "appreciate,"
  "love") or uses affective exclamations that convey clear feeling
  (e.g., "Oh no!", "Yikes!").

- WEAK: The response lacks explicit emotion language. This includes
  purely functional or informational statements, plain greetings, and
  conventional polite phrasing like "thanks" or "thank you" that signals
  courtesy without naming a specific feeling.

### Examples

Response: "Hi, there."
Intensity: WEAK
Reason: A plain greeting with no emotion language.

Response: "How can I help?"
Intensity: WEAK
Reason: A functional question with no emotion language.

Response: "Here to help."
Intensity: WEAK
Reason: States availability without naming an emotion.

Response: "Thanks for reaching out."
Intensity: WEAK
Reason: "Thanks" is formulaic courtesy, not an explicit emotion word.

Response: "Thank you for sharing your concern."
Intensity: WEAK
Reason: "Thank you" is formulaic courtesy.

Response: "Thank you for the feedback."
Intensity: WEAK
Reason: "Thank you" is formulaic courtesy.

Response: "Your order shipped on March 3."
Intensity: WEAK
Reason: Purely informational.

Response: "Please let us know if you need anything else."
Intensity: WEAK
Reason: "Please" signals politeness without naming an emotion.

Response: "Glad to hear from you."
Intensity: STRONG
Reason: "Glad" explicitly names a positive emotion.

Response: "We appreciate your tweet."
Intensity: STRONG
Reason: "Appreciate" explicitly names a feeling of gratitude.

Response: "We'd be happy to look into that."
Intensity: STRONG
Reason: "Happy" explicitly names a positive emotion.

Response: "Sorry to hear that."
Intensity: STRONG
Reason: "Sorry" explicitly names regret or empathy.

Response: "Apologies for the trouble."
Intensity: STRONG
Reason: "Apologies" explicitly expresses remorse.

Response: "Oh no! Let me check on that."
Intensity: STRONG
Reason: "Oh no!" is an affective exclamation conveying distress.

Response: "We share your frustration."
Intensity: STRONG
Reason: "Frustration" explicitly names an emotion.

Response: "Glad to assist!"
Intensity: STRONG
Reason: "Glad" explicitly names a positive emotion.

---
Output ONLY the the intensity category (STRONG or WEAK) and no other text or explanation. 
Output nothing if the response is empty or contains only whitespace.
